All FAQs
Which parts of the shoe can be replaced?

Three modules come off the chassis: the outsole, the upper and the insole. Each is sold separately in every colour and size. The chassis itself is covered by a 5-year warranty and is replaced free if it ever fails.

How do I swap a module?

Every RE:RUN One ships with a module key. The outsole twist-locks at six points; turn the key a quarter turn at each point and lift. The upper rides a hook-and-loop rail with a front lock; release the lock and peel. The insole lifts straight out. First swap takes about five minutes — see our step-by-step guide in the Journal.

How do I find my size?

RE:RUN One runs true to size in US unisex sizing. If you are between sizes, go up half a size. Modules use the same size as your chassis. A printable size chart is on the Shipping & Returns page.

Is RE:RUN One a road or trail shoe?

The standard outsole is tuned for road and light gravel. A lug outsole module for trail is in development for 2027.

How does the return-credit loop work?

Order a replacement module, pack the worn one in the prepaid mailer that comes with every module order and drop it at any post office. Once it reaches the Repair Lab we credit your account: $12 for an outsole, $18 for an upper, $6 for an insole. Credit never expires.

Will replacement modules be sold separately?

Yes. Outsole ($59), upper ($79) and insole ($29) modules are all available now in every colour and size, with or without a complete sneaker order.
